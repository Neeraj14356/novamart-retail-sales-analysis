# NovaMart Retail — Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, and business trends across different regions, product categories, customer types, and sales channels.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to summarize key performance indicators (KPIs) and help management make data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem Statement

NovaMart Retail needs a consolidated view of its sales performance and profitability across different business segments. Management wants to identify top-performing regions, product categories, and sales channels, understand monthly sales trends, and evaluate gross profit performance.

The goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and gross profit.
- Evaluate gross profit margin and cost of goods sold (COGS).
- Compare sales performance across regions and countries.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Analyze sales performance across different sales channels.
- Understand monthly revenue and profitability trends.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains retail sales records with information about order dates, geographical regions, countries, sales channels, customer types, product categories, products, units sold, unit prices, discounts, unit costs, revenue, cost of goods sold, and gross profit.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and reporting.
- **Excel Formulas:** Calculations and KPI development.
- **VLOOKUP & XLOOKUP:** Data lookup and retrieval, where implemented.
- **Pivot Tables:** Summarizing and comparing sales data.
- **Pivot Charts:** Visualizing trends and performance.
- **Slicers:** Interactive filtering of dashboard reports.
- **Dashboard Design:** Presenting business performance through KPIs and charts.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

## Business Questions

1. Which regions generate the highest revenue?
2. Which product categories contribute the most revenue and gross profit?
3. Which sales channel performs best?
4. How do revenue and gross profit change over time?
5. Which products and customer segments perform best?
6. Where can management investigate opportunities to improve profitability?
